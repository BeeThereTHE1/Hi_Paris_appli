Learn Deep Learning differently with Hi! Paris

Hi! Paris Playground is an interactive and engaging platform specifically designed to demystify the world of artificial intelligence and neural networks.

For students: Run algorithms in real time, experiment with architectures, understand the role of hidden layers, and watch how your data is classified before your eyes.

For teachers: Easily create custom exercises, generate complex datasets, and share them with the community to help guide your students in understanding the mathematics behind AI.
